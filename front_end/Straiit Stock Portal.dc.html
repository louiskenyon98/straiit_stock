About

A clearing house for European apparel and footwear stock.

Straiit buys end-of-season carryover, cancelled wholesale orders and closed retail accounts from European brands and factories, then sells it whole to importers and wholesalers outside the brand's own retail channel. Every lot is counted in our warehouse before it is listed, so the SKU sheet you request against is the stock that exists.

We are a principal, not a broker. Stock is bought and held on our own account, which is why we can hold cartons for you while a quote runs and why counts do not change after you commit.

01 · Grading

A-grade is brand-new with original labelling and packaging. B-grade covers ex-display, marked or repacked goods and is always listed as a separate SKU line, never mixed into an A-grade count.

02 · Territory

Some brands carry territory restrictions. These are confirmed in writing at quotation, before any deposit. We will not sell into a restricted market.

03 · Terms of sale

Sold as seen, per SKU units, minimum order per lot. 30% deposit on order confirmation, balance against warehouse release. EXW as standard; FOB and CIF quoted on request.

04 · Inspection

You or your agent may inspect any lot in the warehouse before committing. Sample cartons can be shipped at cost.

05 · Documents

Commercial invoice, packing list per carton, EUR.1 or certificate of origin where applicable, and photographs of the pallets as loaded.
